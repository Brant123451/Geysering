# OpenFOAM 2-D Case B

This directory contains the executed two-dimensional compressible air-water
VOF model for the Vasconcelos and Wright (2011) condition
`Dt=12.7 mm`, `Ha0=0.610 m`, and `Yfs,0=0.356 m`. The quantitative targets are
the centre panels of Fig. 6 (pressure) and Fig. 8 (free surface and air-water
interface).

## Experimental alignment

Primary source:

> Vasconcelos, J. G., and Wright, S. J. (2011), "Geysering Generated by
> Large Air Pockets Released through Water-Filled Ventilation Shafts,"
> *Journal of Hydraulic Engineering*, 137(5), 543-555,
> [doi:10.1061/(ASCE)HY.1943-7900.0000332](https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)HY.1943-7900.0000332).

The model uses the published apparatus dimensions:

| Quantity | Value used |
|---|---:|
| Horizontal pipe diameter, `D` | 0.094 m |
| Upstream air-pocket length | 0.546 m |
| Valve coordinate | `x=0.546 m` |
| Tower centre coordinate | `x=3.516 m` |
| Tower distance downstream of valve | 2.970 m |
| Closed pipe downstream of tower centre | 0.490 m |
| Tower diameter, `Dt` | 0.0127 m |
| Tower height above the pipe crown, `L` | 0.610 m |
| Initial tower level above the crown, `Yfs,0` | 0.356 m |
| Initial air gauge-pressure head, `Ha0` | 0.610 m |
| Pressure transducer downstream of valve | 1.070 m |
| Pressure transducer coordinate | `(1.616, -0.043, 0) m` |

The pipe crown is `y=0.047 m`, so the initial tower free surface is
`y=0.403 m`. With `rho_w=998.2 kg/m3`, `g=9.81 m/s2`, and
`p_atm=101325 Pa`, the initial air-pocket pressure is
`p_atm+rho_w*g*Ha0=107298.329 Pa`. The valve is represented as an open
internal mesh interface at `t=0`; the phase and pressure discontinuity on its
two sides models instantaneous opening.

The comparison follows the paper's nondimensionalization without a fitted
time shift:

```text
T*    = t sqrt(g Dt) / L
H*    = (p - p_atm) / (rho_w g L)
Yfs*  = (yfs  - y_crown) / L
Yint* = (yint - y_crown) / L
Dt*   = Dt / D = 0.1351
```

The experimental CSV files are digitized from the open-preview rasters of the
same paper. `digitized/source_metadata.json` records the URLs, SHA-256 hashes,
axis calibration, and corroborating literature. Fig. 6 is a raster-derived
three-trace envelope and median, not raw transducer data. Fig. 8 marker centres
were retained by repetition; its overlapping filled free-surface markers are
reported as one aggregate centreline. See `digitized/README.md` for the
method and uncertainty.

## Changes from the Case A template

The compatible OpenFOAM v2512 solver, phase thermodynamics, atmospheric tower
outlet, wall conditions, bounded VOF schemes, and probe-based postprocessing
were reused from Case A. Case B required independent changes to:

- tower width: `0.0571 -> 0.0127 m`;
- initial air head: `0.305 -> 0.610 m`;
- tower-junction mesh and 5 mm centreline probe spacing;
- Case B time scale `sqrt(g*0.0127)/0.610`;
- 9 s physical duration (`T*=5.208`);
- Fig. 6/Fig. 8 centre-panel experimental references and event metrics.

No model coefficient or event-time offset was adjusted to improve agreement.

## Numerical model and boundary conditions

- OpenFOAM v2512 `compressibleInterFoam`;
- ideal-gas air and slightly compressible `perfectFluid` water;
- water `rho0=998.2 kg/m3`, squared acoustic speed
  `R=2.2e6 m2/s2` (`c` about 1483 m/s);
- laminar transport, initial temperature 293.15 K, and surface tension
  0.072 N/m;
- no-slip closed pipe/tower walls and two-dimensional `empty` front/back
  boundaries;
- atmospheric pressure outlet at the tower rim, with air admitted on reverse
  flow;
- adaptive time step with `maxCo=0.20`, `maxAlphaCo=0.15`, and
  `maxDeltaT=5e-4 s`.

Hydrostatic initialization uses `p_rgh=p-rho(g dot x)`. The tower water
column has constant `p_rgh=105271.314 Pa`; the atmosphere-connected air above
`y=0.403 m` is initialized at atmospheric absolute pressure.

## Mesh and executed checks

`blockMesh` creates 51,984 orthogonal hexahedral cells in a 4 mm deep
two-dimensional extrusion. In-plane edge lengths range from 1.5875 to 4.0 mm;
the narrow tower has eight cells across its diameter and 306 cells vertically.

The executed `checkMesh -allTopology -allGeometry` result was `Mesh OK`:

- maximum non-orthogonality: 0 degrees;
- maximum skewness: `2.78e-12`;
- maximum aspect ratio: `1.992`;
- one connected region with positive cell volumes.

The serial 0.02 s smoke run reached its requested end time with final
`Co_max=0.0855`, interface `Co_max=0.0825`, and
`alpha.water=[-2.62e-19, 1]`. Its recorded values are in
`outputs/smoke_run_metrics.json`.

## Run and reproduce

Run commands from this directory:

```bash
chmod +x Allrun Allrun.resume Allrun.smoke
./Allrun.smoke
OPENFOAM_NP=4 ./Allrun
```

`OPENFOAM_NP` is optional and capped at six ranks. `Allrun` copies `0.orig`,
generates and checks the mesh, initializes fields, decomposes the case, runs
the solver, and executes the comparison script.

To resume an interrupted decomposed run from its newest retained checkpoint:

```bash
./Allrun.resume
```

To regenerate only the reference data or comparison artifacts:

```bash
python3 digitize_reference.py
python3 postprocess_compare.py
```

The digitizer requires NumPy, Pillow, and SciPy; comparison plotting requires
NumPy and Matplotlib.

## Executed full-run result

The four-rank run completed 9.0 s (`T*=5.2077`) in 2,544 s wall time and
exited normally. At the final step, `Co_max=0.1751`, interface
`Co_max=0.1751`, and `alpha.water=[-2.44e-19, 1]`.

| Comparison (no time shift) | Experiment | OpenFOAM | Error/result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Mean pressure plateau, `1<=T*<=3.5` | 0.7655 | 0.8108 | bias `+0.0453 H*` |
| Pressure trace | - | - | RMSE `0.2681 H*` |
| Pressure release at `H*=0.4` | `T*=4.0149` | not reached by `5.2077` | delay `>1.1928 T*` (`>2.061 s`) |
| Free-surface rim arrival, `Yfs*=0.99` | `T*=3.9361` | not reached | delay `>1.2716 T*` (`>2.198 s`) |
| Interface at `Yint*=0.85` | median `T*=4.1396` | not reached | delay `>1.0681 T*` (`>1.846 s`) |
| Free-surface trajectory | - | max `Yfs*=0.8429` | RMSE `0.1976 Y*` |
| Interface trajectory | - | max `Yint*=0.5648` | RMSE `0.3437 Y*` |

Thus the model reproduces a pressurized plateau to within `0.045 H*`, but its
large pressure oscillations and delayed venting produce a high full-trace
RMSE. The simulated air pocket enters the tower and raises the interface, yet
the water surface does not reach the rim in the experimental comparison
window; unlike the experiment, this 2-D model does not geyser. The `>` timing
errors are right-censored lower bounds at the simulation end, not fitted event
times. Machine-readable values and exact definitions are in
`outputs/openfoam_2d_metrics.json`.

directories, `postProcessing/`, and verbose `log.*` files are deliberately
excluded from version control. They are reproducible and/or resumable working
data, not compact review artifacts.

## Interpretation limits

The planar model preserves the published diameters but cannot also preserve
the circular flow-area ratio: its junction ratio is `Dt/D=0.1351`, while the
physical circular ratio is `(Dt/D)^2=0.01825`. This substantially changes
air/water exchange at the tower. A 2-D laminar VOF calculation also cannot
resolve the observed three-dimensional annular wall film, breakup, or
entrainment. Finally, the domain ends at the open rim, so `Yfs*>=0.99`
identifies water reaching the rim but does not resolve the above-rim jet.
These limitations make the calculation a reproducible 2-D diagnostic, not a
geometry-exact validation model.
